ABOUT US

Thirty years of turning creative ambition into working careers in animation, VFX, gaming and design.

We don't teach software. We build artists.

Arena Animation has been part of India's media and entertainment education industry since 1996. Established by Aptech, we train students in animation, VFX, gaming and digital design through industry-forward courses, hands-on projects and mentorship from working professionals.

Alongside technical skill, our programmes build the interpersonal ability every studio expects: communication, collaboration, working to deadlines and presenting your own work with confidence.

Think different. Think animation.

Vision

To be the institute that shapes India's creative workforce, as the country becomes a global hub for animation, gaming and digital content.

Mission

To help students achieve financial independence through job-ready skills, so every graduate leaves with a portfolio and a career.

Animation is not a genre. It is the craft of
making inanimate objects move.

How an artist approaches the software matters more than the software itself. We blend an artistic approach into our teaching method so students leave able to think, not only to operate a toolset.
